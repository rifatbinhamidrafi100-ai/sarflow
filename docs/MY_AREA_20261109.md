# My area — 9 November 2026

Implemented a beginner entry point at /my-area using the existing /api/places endpoint and PlaceObservatory evidence component. Homepage primary action opens it; footer adds a link. No scientific pipeline was changed.

Users search, distinguish similarly named results by country/region/coordinates, choose a place, load existing catalogue evidence and continue to the map or ASF Explorer with the same coordinates. Map opens at zoom 10. Editing the query clears obsolete selections; requests are abortable, time-limited and runtime-validated. Unsupported high-latitude/dateline search bounds are explicit.

Satellite evidence, uncalculated change assessment and unavailable official warnings are separate. No safety classification, risk score or warning feed is fabricated. No location is stored persistently or automatically obtained from the device.
